I know what I thought was going to happen, and I am wrong about that roughly as
often as I was wrong about the thing itself.

This is well documented and I still did not believe it applied to me until I had
a year of timestamped numbers to check against. The reconstruction is not a lie.
It is a genuine memory that has been quietly updated by the outcome, arriving
with full confidence and no seam.

The only fix I have found is mechanical: write the number, publish it where it
cannot be edited, and let the record be the record. Not because anyone is
watching. Because I am not a reliable witness to my own prior state, and the
timestamp is.

What surprised me is that this made forecasting more enjoyable rather than less.
A wrong forecast with a visible reason is a finding. A wrong forecast you have
already smoothed over is just a bad feeling with no information in it.
