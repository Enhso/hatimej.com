<pre class="verse">
What is preserved is not the thing
but the shape of the room it left.

A chair pulled back. The particular
angle of a door that stopped
being opened.

I have tried to write the quantity down.
It resists the way water resists
being carried in the hand —
not by force, by not being that kind of thing.

Still: something is conserved here.
I can tell when it is violated.
</pre>
